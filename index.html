Privacy Policy

MYCHANGE — Currency Converter

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MYCHANGE — Currency Converter (the “App”), package name com.mychange.currencyconverter, handles information when you use the App.

1. Information stored on your device

The App does not require you to create an account. Currency selections, app preferences, favorite currency pairs and conversion history are stored locally on your device using the device’s application storage. This information is not sent to the developer and is not synchronized to a developer-operated server.

2. Currency exchange-rate requests

The App connects to the Frankfurter service to retrieve reference exchange rates. When your device makes a request, the service and network infrastructure may receive technical information such as your IP address, request time and standard connection metadata. This is necessary to deliver the requested exchange-rate data.

MYCHANGE does not send the amounts you enter, your conversion history, favorite currency pairs or app preferences as part of its exchange-rate requests.

Frankfurter is a third-party service. Its handling of connection data is governed by its own privacy information and policies. Learn more at https://www.frankfurter.dev/.

3. Analytics and advertising

The current version of the App does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. It does not display advertisements.

4. Sharing and sale of information

The developer does not sell your personal information. The developer does not receive the locally stored conversion history, favorite pairs or app preferences. Technical connection information may be processed by the third-party exchange-rate service as described above.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by the App remains on your device until you delete it within the App (where that option is available), clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. The developer does not maintain a copy of this locally stored information.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-purpose currency utility and is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Local App information is stored using the platform’s application storage. Network requests to retrieve exchange rates use HTTPS where supported by the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can stop the App from making exchange-rate requests by discontinuing use of the online features or uninstalling the App. Depending on your location, you may have privacy rights under applicable law. Because the developer does not receive the locally stored information described in this policy, you can manage or delete that information directly on your device.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App’s features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the latest version was published. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the App’s privacy practices, contact the developer at: nikidevstudio@gmail.com.

Note: This policy describes the current version of MYCHANGE without advertising or analytics SDKs. If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync or other third-party SDKs are added in a future release, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be reviewed and updated before that release.
